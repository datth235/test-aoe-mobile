# Asset credits

- World tiles, trees, buildings, units, selection markers and rally flag: original procedural artwork generated at load by `src/render/createTextures.ts`. No external artwork pack is included.
- Nunito variable font (`public/assets/Nunito.ttf`): The Nunito Project Authors, [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/nunito), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license text is included as `public/assets/Nunito-OFL.txt`.

All artwork is packed into one generated canvas texture atlas during preload. Downloaded assets total less than 0.3 MB.
